Car accident on intersection of Park Ave and Township Line Road

At 5:00pm on September 14th, 2026, there was a collision between 2 pickup trucks on Township Line Road and Park Avenue in Wycombe, a border town between Wrightstown and Buckingham Townships. The car approaching from park avenue hit another pickup truck heading northeast on Township Line road, and was hit in the side. It coasted about 20 yards to a nearby driveway. The first car, a red pickup truck, was totaled. A local resident called 911 and the Newtown police came in about 5 minutes. It was discovered that no one was hurt in the inccident, but debris was thrown as far as 25 yards away. After inital assessment by the police, a flatbed truck belonging to Newtown Auto LLC arrived to pick up the totaled vehicle. A cleanup effort ensued to pick up the scattered debris, with police putting most of it in the back of the truck. The rest of it, collected by a few civilians, were later thrown into a dumpster. One of those civilians, Paster Joshua Brownfield of First Baptist Church Wycombe soon discovered that the driver of the totaled vehicle had early stage dementia, and assisted him in getting an Uber home to Morrisville, which is 1 hour in drive time from the scene.

-Luke Brownfield
